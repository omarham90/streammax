Frequently asked questions

Read the basics before contacting support.

How do I confirm a plan?
Use the enquiry button for the duration you want. Ask for the exact features, supported content, total price, payment method, renewal terms and refund policy in writing before paying.
Which devices are supported?
Support depends on the authorised service and compatible app. Send the model of your TV, streaming device or phone to confirm compatibility.
How does the reseller option work?
Ask for written information about the provider, panel access, credit rules, permitted content, support, additional charges and proof of distribution authorisation. Do not purchase until you understand the terms.
What is your refund policy?
Request the current written refund and cancellation terms before making a payment. Any policy must comply with the laws that apply to the sale.
Are currency conversions live?
No. Converted prices are estimates using reference rates built into this page. The final price and currency must be confirmed before payment.
